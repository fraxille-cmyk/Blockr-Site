FEATURES

A whole world inside your phone — designed to keep you off it.

01

You are the sun.

Open Blockr and you'll meet a living solar system. You're the sun. Five planets orbit you — Creativity, Clarity, Focus, Energy, Discipline. Every minute spent scrolling chips away at them. Stay in control and they grow brighter.

  • Watch your pillars react in real time
  • See exactly how much damage today caused
  • Edit which apps count as “bad” in one tap
02

Set a plan you'll actually keep.

A daily screentime goal, a 10‑minute grace buffer, and per‑category app limits. When the limit hits, the app locks. No willpower required.

  • Daily goal with grace buffer
  • Category limits for Social, Games, more
  • Hard blocks when you cross the line
03

Honest analytics. Maybe brutal.

Today, week, month, year. See the truth: hours scrolled, days lost, how far over goal you went. The numbers don't lie — and they're hard to ignore.

  • “You have lost control” when you over‑run
  • Lifetime days‑on‑phone counter
  • Compare yourself against your own goal
04

Focus modes & schedules.

Quick‑block on demand. Reusable modes for Work, Sleep, Study, Exercise. Schedules that fire on the days you choose. And a Bad Apps list with a shared daily timer — burn it and they're gone for the day.
